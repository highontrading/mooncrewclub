FAQ

Before you apply.

Who is this trip for?

People who love psytrance and big-night energy, and want it without the queues, crowds and planning. Couples, friends and solo guests are all welcome.

Can I come alone?

Yes. The crew meets in a WhatsApp group before the trip, and we match room partners for twin sharing. If you'd prefer a private room, ask us and we'll confirm availability and price.

Do I need a visa for Thailand?

Indian passport holders can currently enter Thailand visa-free. Your passport should be valid for at least six months. We check the latest rules with you before booking.

How does payment work?

A 50% advance locks your seat, and the balance is due by 28 November. Indian taxes on international tour packages (GST and TCS) are shown separately on your invoice; TCS can be claimed back when you file your income-tax return.

What if the trip doesn't happen?

If the trip isn't confirmed by 28 November, your advance is refunded in full. Our full cancellation terms are shared with you in writing before you pay.

Are flights included?

The trip starts at Koh Samui airport. We can book your flights from India as an add-on; tell us in your application.
